5 Creative Ways to Pitch AirVault to B2B & Defense Buyers

Enterprise buyers in aerospace, defense, nuclear, and semiconductor manufacturing are skeptical of slideware. Here are five high-impact live demonstration stunts designed to close technical and executive stakeholders on the spot.

PITCH IDEA 01 // THE PHYSICAL STUNT

The “Scissors / Unplug the Ethernet” Live Wire-Tap

Start your B2B demo by opening Wireshark on the host laptop and physically pulling the Ethernet cable out of the wall (or switching to Airplane Mode). Run the 1,420-page query while the packet monitor reads 0.00 B/s WAN. Nothing convinces a CISO faster than physical proof.

PITCH IDEA 02 // THE PROSPECT CHALLENGE

“Bring Your Worst 1980s Coffee-Stained Blueprint”

Invite the prospect’s Chief Engineer to bring a skewed, multi-column PDF or scanned TIFF schematic on a USB drive. Drop it into AirVault live and let Docling + Surya OCR reconstruct the Markdown table and bounding boxes in under 15 seconds.

PITCH IDEA 03 // THE SECURITY DEMO

“Two Smartcards, One Prompt” ABAC Proof

Carry two hardware YubiKeys/CAC smartcards in your pocket—one provisioned as SECRET//ITAR and one as SUBCONTRACTOR CUI. Tap each card and run the exact same prompt to demonstrate instant pre-LLM vector chunk redaction.

PITCH IDEA 04 // THE CFO ROI HOOK

The “Footnote 17 on Page 1,184” Stopwatch Race

Ask a senior engineer how long it takes to verify whether a MIL-SPEC fastener on Page 1,184 was superseded by a later Engineering Change Order across 40 PDF folders (typically 3–6 hours). Show AirVault linking both pages with bounding boxes in 1.4 seconds.

PITCH IDEA 05 // FRICTIONLESS PROCUREMENT

The “Keep the NVMe Drives” 30-Day Pilot Guarantee

Eliminate data-sanitization procurement delays by shipping the portable USB NVMe SSD (or 2U/4U appliance) with a contractual guarantee: even if they don’t buy after the 30-day pilot, their security team physically keeps the NVMe drives inside their vault.
